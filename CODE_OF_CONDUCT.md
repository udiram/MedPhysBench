# Code of conduct

We are committed to a professional, inclusive, evidence-focused community. Participants must:

- critique methods and evidence rather than people;
- disclose conflicts of interest;
- protect patient privacy and restricted evaluation material;
- avoid overstating benchmark results or clinical implications;
- respect contributors, reviewers, and model providers.

Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, credential sharing, deliberate benchmark leakage, and unsafe
clinical claims are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content or participation that violates
these expectations.
